Work on the project at any stage.

Collaborate without divergence.

Compare geometry extremely fast.

Every object is an entity. Every version is immutable.

Objects, layers, materials and block definitions are keyed by their Rhino GUID. Each version is a manifest of component blobs and references, addressed by its SHA-256 hash.
